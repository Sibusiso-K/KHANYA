1 · Belt hyperspectral camera

SECONDS · EVERY TONNE

VNIR + SWIR line scan over the mill-feed belt, edge PC beside it. Reads the gangue and alteration minerals that set hardness, reagent demand and recovery.

Early warning: “ore change — harder, lower recovery, more lime”.

2 · REEFPRINT microscope

WITHIN THE SHIFT

A polished section from the cyclone overflow or flotation feed, on any lab microscope camera. Sees the payable sulphides and how they are locked.

Grind and reagent advice — act · verify · conservative default — with a reason and an audit record.

3 · QEMSCAN & lab assays

DAYS · THE TEACHER

Automated mineralogy and metallurgical tests on composites. Too slow to steer a shift, exact enough to label training data for both instruments.

Re-trains the belt and microscope models; takes the samples REEFPRINT refuses.

Control room: belt flag + microscope advice + audit trail on one screen. Only a setpoint a person approves reaches the plant (OPC UA).
